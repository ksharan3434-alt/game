Privacy Policy
Last updated: 7 October 2026
No real money is involved. This app does not accept deposits or payments, does not sell coins or tokens, and does not offer withdrawals or cash prizes. Tokens have no cash value.

1. About this app

Colour Prediction is a game played with virtual play tokens, made for entertainment only.

2. No real money

  • You cannot deposit money, pay, or buy tokens in this app.
  • You cannot withdraw money. There are no cash prizes.
  • Tokens cannot be exchanged for money, goods or services outside this app.
  • Rewards such as badges, characters and skills exist only inside this app and have no cash value.
  • We never ask for your bank, UPI, wallet or card details. If anyone asks you for money in the name of this app, it is not us. Do not pay them.

3. Information we collect

  • Your name and email address, and the password you choose (handled by Firebase Authentication, so we cannot see your password).
  • A user ID (UID) and your token balance.
  • Your game history, reward requests and the messages you send to support.
  • Small settings saved on your device, such as which notifications you have read.

4. How we use it

  • To run the game, keep your balance and show the leaderboard.
  • To handle rewards and reply to your support messages.
  • To keep the app safe and block accounts that misuse it.

5. Who can see your information

  • Other players can see your name and token count on the leaderboard.
  • The app admin can see your name, UID, balance, support messages and reward requests. The admin can change a token balance and block accounts that break the rules.
  • We do not sell your information.

6. Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore). It is shared with Firebase only to run the app, or if the law requires it.

7. Age

This app is only for people aged 18 or older.

8. Play responsibly

Games like this can be addictive. Play only for fun and take regular breaks. If it stops being fun, stop playing.

9. Your choices

To correct or delete your data, send a message from the Support section of the app.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available in the app, under My Account.

12. Contact

Use the Support section of the app to contact us.
